# Getting Started with Cortex

Welcome to Cortex — your team's collective AI brain. This guide takes you from signup to your first streamed answer in under three minutes.

## Creating your account

Sign up with Google or an email and password at app.cortex.app/signup (the whole signup takes under a minute). No credit card is required for the Starter plan. If you received an invite email, click the invite link first — your team membership is created automatically when your account is confirmed.

## Your first chat

After signup you land on the chat workspace. Type a question into the composer at the bottom and press Enter (or Cmd+Enter). Answers stream token-by-token; most first tokens arrive in about 1.2 seconds on broadband.

Four suggestion cards on the empty state help you start: drafting a launch email, summarizing a document, brainstorming campaign ideas, and analyzing a CSV.

## Picking a model

The model picker sits above the composer. Cortex Flash is the default: fast and inexpensive, ideal for drafts and everyday questions. Cortex Pro handles harder reasoning. Cortex Reason is the deep-thinking model for complex analysis. Your team admin can set a different team default.

## Organizing conversations

Conversations appear in the left sidebar grouped by Today, This week, and Older. Hover any conversation to pin it, rename it, move it to a folder, or delete it. Deleted conversations rest in trash for 30 days before permanent removal.

## Next steps

- Save a great prompt as a template with the "Save as template" action on any message.
- Invite teammates from the Team page — Starter includes 3 seats.
- Open the Usage page to see your token consumption after your first chats.
