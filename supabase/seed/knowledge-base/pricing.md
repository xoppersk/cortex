# Cortex Pricing

Cortex uses transparent per-seat pricing. Every plan is billed per active seat per month, with no hidden token markups — model costs are included in your seat price up to generous fair-use limits.

## Plans

**Starter — $0.** For individuals and tiny teams trying Cortex. Includes 3 seats, 100k tokens per user per month, community prompt templates, and 30-day conversation retention.

**Pro costs $20 per seat per month.** For growing teams. Includes 2M tokens per user per month, shared team prompt libraries, the knowledge base (1 KB, 500 documents), usage analytics, and 90-day retention.

**Team costs $35 per seat per month.** For companies that need governance. Everything in Pro, plus unlimited knowledge bases, audit log, API keys, SSO-ready admin controls, custom data-retention windows (30/90/365 days), and priority support.

Annual billing saves 20% on Pro and Team.

## Per-seat pricing

A seat is one active team member. Deactivating a member immediately frees their seat and stops billing for it at the next invoice (prorated). Invited-but-not-yet-accepted members do not consume seats.

## Overage behavior

If a user exceeds their monthly token allowance, the team admin chooses the behavior in Team Settings: **alert-only** (the default — admins get an email at 80% and 100%) or **hard stop** (chat pauses for that user until the next cycle or a budget increase). There are never surprise overage charges: overages are either blocked or alerted, never silently billed.

## Refunds

Monthly plans can be cancelled any time from the billing page; you keep access until the end of the billing period. Annual plans are refundable within the first 30 days.
