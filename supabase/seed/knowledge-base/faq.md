# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you train models on my data?

No. Your content powers your features (history, search, retrieval) and is never used for model training.

## Which model should I use?

Cortex Flash for speed and cost, Cortex Pro for harder reasoning, Cortex Reason for the toughest analysis. The picker shows cost hints per model.

## Can I use Cortex on my phone?

Yes — the web app is fully responsive. The conversation list becomes the default view, the sidebar opens as a sheet, and the composer docks above the keyboard. There are no native iOS/Android apps in v1.

## How do citations work?

Knowledge-base answers cite the exact chunks used: [1], [2], and so on. Click a citation to open the Sources panel with the chunk text, document name, section, and similarity score. You can thumb up/down any answer and flag bad citations for admin review.

## What happens when I leave a team?

Your access ends immediately. Your personal templates stay yours; team templates you authored remain with the team (attributed to "a former member" if your account is deleted). Your usage history remains for the team's accounting.

## Is there an API?

Yes, on the Team plan: create an API key, then POST to /api/v1/chat with a bearer token. Keys support streaming (SSE) and JSON responses, with per-key usage attribution.

## Do you offer SSO/SAML?

Not in v1 — Google OAuth and email/password only. SSO is on the roadmap for Team plans.

## How do I get support?

Starter: community forum. Pro: email, 48-hour response. Team: priority support, 4-hour response. All plans: status.cortex.app and the in-app docs.
