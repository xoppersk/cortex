# Choosing a Model

Cortex offers three models behind one picker. They differ in capability, speed, and cost — pick the cheapest one that does the job.

## Cortex Flash — fast & cheap

The default. Best for drafts, summaries, brainstorming, and everyday questions. Responds in about a second with the lowest token cost. If you are unsure, start here — and switch to Cortex Pro when a task needs harder reasoning.

## Cortex Pro — balanced reasoning

For harder tasks: structured analysis, multi-step instructions, nuanced writing. Roughly 10x the token cost of Flash, noticeably smarter on complex prompts.

## Cortex Reason — deep thinking

For the hardest problems: debugging thorny code, strategy trade-offs, careful data analysis. The slowest and most expensive; reserve it for work where quality matters more than speed.

## Cost guidance

Token costs are metered per message and visible in the message footer (e.g. "1,204 tokens · 3.2s · Cortex Flash"). The Usage dashboard breaks cost down by model so admins can see which models drive spend. A common pattern: set the team default to Flash and let power users switch to Pro or Reason per conversation.

## Temperature

Temperature (0–2, default 0.7) controls randomness. Lower values (0.2–0.4) give focused, deterministic answers — good for extraction and classification. Higher values (0.9–1.2) give more creative, varied output — good for brainstorming. Your personal default lives in Settings; the team default in Team Settings.
