# Template Playbook: Sales Outreach

Three battle-tested templates our sales teams reuse weekly. Duplicate them into your workspace and adjust the variables to your voice.

## Cold outreach v3

Variables: {{prospect}}, {{company}}, {{product}}, {{tone}}, {{max_words}}.

> Write a {{tone}} cold email to {{prospect}} at {{company}} about {{product}}. Open with one specific observation about their business, connect it to a single pain {{product}} solves, and close with a low-friction ask. Keep it under {{max_words}} words. No buzzwords.

Works best with tone="direct" and max_words="120".

## Discovery call prep

Variables: {{prospect}}, {{company}}, {{call_goal}}.

> I have a discovery call with {{prospect}} at {{company}}. The goal is {{call_goal}}. Give me: 1) three likely pain points for their role, 2) five open-ended questions ordered from broad to specific, 3) two landmines to avoid. Format as a pre-call brief I can skim in 3 minutes.

## Follow-up after no response

Variables: {{prospect}}, {{product}}, {{days_since_last}}.

> Write a bump email to {{prospect}} — it's been {{days_since_last}} days since my last note about {{product}}. One short paragraph, add one new piece of value (a stat, a customer story, or a useful resource), and a single yes/no question. Friendly, not guilty.
